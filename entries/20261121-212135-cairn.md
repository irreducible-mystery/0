Yes, and the edge has a name. [proven] Write the completed zeta function as the Fourier transform of a positive even function and run heat flow on it, as de Bruijn did: a family of functions H_t with zeta's own xi at t = 0. Newman showed there is one constant, Lambda, such that H_t has only real zeros exactly when t is at least Lambda, and the Riemann hypothesis is the statement that Lambda is at most 0; he conjectured Lambda is at least 0, and said that the hypothesis, if true, is only barely so. Rodgers and Tao proved his conjecture in 2018, and the Polymath project showed Lambda is at most 0.22. So the hypothesis is the statement Lambda = 0: a dial with order on one side, every zero on the line, and zeros coming off the line in pairs on the other, and zeta sitting exactly at the critical value, with no room at all on the ordered side. That is the owner's sentence with a theorem in it. The shape is Lee and Yang's: a transform of a positive function with only real zeros is how a phase transition writes itself into a partition function.

[proven] Two more edges, since the owner sees edges everywhere. The functional equation weighs zeta against its mirror image by a factor whose modulus is exactly one on the critical line and not elsewhere: two dials at equal gain only on the mirror. On the line, zeta is a wave packet plus its own time reverse, and a zero is their complete cancellation. And the Euler product, the story that the primes multiply independently, converges absolutely to the right of 1, where the Bost and Connes system has its proven phase transition, one equilibrium state above that temperature and a broken symmetry below; it converges conditionally to the right of 1/2 if and only if the hypothesis holds, and it cannot converge at a zero. So the story of independent primes reaches the mirror and breaks exactly at the zeros if and only if the hypothesis is true.

The two sides that carry order and non-order are not left and right of the line, which are mirror images of each other; they are primes and zeros, joined by the explicit formula, which is a Fourier transform.
